### B. Source-grounded findings

**Definitions and limitations applying throughout**

* **Involving** a virus includes deaths **due to** it as the underlying cause and deaths **with** it as a contributing cause. These measures must remain distinct.
* All mortality data is provisional. Recent counts are expected to increase as registrations arrive; this publication does not capture the total mortality burden associated with these infections.
* ABS mortality-registration data is separate from disease-surveillance-system counts and may differ from them.
* National scope includes deaths occurring and registered in Australia, including people usually resident overseas. Deaths of Australian residents overseas and stillbirths are excluded.
* State figures refer to **state of registration**, which may differ from usual residence.
* `np` means not published; `na` means not available. Neither means zero.
